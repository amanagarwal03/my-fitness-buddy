## Full description (max 4000)
```
My Fitness Buddy is your accountability partner for eating well and training hard — all in one simple app.

📸 SNAP A MEAL, GET THE NUMBERS
Point your camera at any meal and get an instant estimate of calories, protein, carbs, fat, and key micronutrients. No tedious searching through food databases. You can also scan product barcodes for packaged foods.

🎯 HIT YOUR DAILY GOALS
Set your calorie and macro targets and watch your progress fill up through the day. See exactly where you stand at a glance.

🏋️ LOG WORKOUTS THE EASY WAY
Track your sessions exercise by exercise — sets, weights, and reps — with quick + / – steppers and your last workout's numbers pre-filled so you just adjust and go. Switch between kg and lbs anytime. Time your sessions, pause and resume, and log cardio with speed and incline.

📈 SEE YOURSELF GET STRONGER
Per-exercise and per-muscle-group progress charts show whether you're moving up or plateauing, across daily, weekly, and monthly views.

📅 A CALENDAR THAT REMEMBERS
Tap any day to see what you ate and how you trained. Workout days are marked so your consistency is easy to see.

🤝 SHARE WITH A COACH
Generate a share code to give a coach or training partner read-only access to your meals and workouts — including a calendar view of your full history. Revoke access anytime.

⚖️ BMI & BODY METRICS
Track your height and weight and see your BMI and category update over time.

Your data is private and synced securely to your account so it's there on every device.

Start tracking in under a minute. My Fitness Buddy — your accountability partner.
```
