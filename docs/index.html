Privacy Policy — My Fitness Buddy

Effective date: June 20, 2026 · Last updated: June 20, 2026

My Fitness Buddy ("the app", "we", "us") is a personal fitness and nutrition tracking app. This policy explains what we collect, how it is used, and the choices you have. Questions: bestaman03@gmail.com.

Who runs this app

My Fitness Buddy is operated by an individual developer (Aman Agarwal). It is not affiliated with Google, Supabase, or OpenFoodFacts.

Information we collect

  • Account information. Your email address and a password, used to create and secure your account. Passwords are handled by our authentication provider (Supabase) and stored salted/hashed — we never see your plain-text password.
  • Profile and body metrics. Optional height, weight, sex, date of birth, and unit preference (kg/lbs). Used for BMI and tracking.
  • Nutrition data. Meals you log — names, estimated calories and macro/micronutrients, timestamps, and optional meal photos.
  • Workout data. Exercises, sets (weight, reps), cardio entries (duration, speed, incline), and session durations and dates.
  • Sharing data. If you use coach-sharing, a share code and a record of which accounts you've granted read-only access to (and vice versa).

We do not collect contacts, precise location, advertising identifiers, or browsing history. The app contains no third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.

Device permissions

  • Camera — to photograph meals and scan product barcodes, only when you initiate it.
  • Photos / media — to pick an existing meal photo from your gallery.

We do not scan your photo library in the background.

How we use your information

  • To provide nutrition tracking, BMI, workout logging, and progress.
  • To estimate the nutrition of a meal from its photo (see third-party services).
  • To look up product nutrition when you scan a barcode.
  • To enable the optional coach-sharing feature you control.
  • To keep your account secure and synced across your devices.

We do not sell your personal information and do not use it for advertising.

Third-party services

  • Supabase — backend, authentication, database, and photo storage. Meal photos are kept in a private bucket and accessed only via short-lived signed links. supabase.com/privacy
  • Google Gemini API — when you analyze a meal photo, the image is sent to Google's Gemini model to estimate nutrition, then the result returns to the app. Gemini API terms, Google Privacy. Depending on the API tier, Google may process/retain submitted content per its terms.
  • OpenFoodFacts — when you scan a barcode, only the barcode number is sent to retrieve public product data. No personal data is sent. openfoodfacts.org/privacy

Sharing with other users (coach feature)

Sharing is optional and user-initiated. If you share a code (or redeem one), the recipient gets read-only access to your meals, workouts, and body metrics. You can revoke access at any time in the app, which stops their access immediately.

Data retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account exists. You can request deletion of your account and all associated data by emailing bestaman03@gmail.com from your account's email address; we will delete it within 30 days. Deleting your account removes your profile, meals, meal photos, workouts, and sharing records.

Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). Database access is protected by row-level security so you can only read/write your own data (plus anything explicitly shared with you). Meal photos are stored privately and served via expiring signed URLs.

Children

My Fitness Buddy is intended for adults and is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves and will revise the "Last updated" date above.

Contact

Questions or requests (including data deletion): bestaman03@gmail.com
